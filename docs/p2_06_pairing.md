Share the printed `pairing_summary.json` and `identity_coverage.json` for review.

The adapter verifies snapshot and inventory hashes, reads all recording envelope rows, and builds a full outer grouping by source-relative filename stem. `coverage.jsonl` records component multiplicity for genuine audio, four TTS families and transcripts. `candidate_edges.jsonl` records candidate same-stem links; `verified_edges.jsonl` is intentionally empty until relationships receive appropriate evidence. Every audio row receives a pending pairing disposition. No split or segment is produced.

Transcript files are checked against their recorded byte hashes. The audit records UTF-8/BOM, empty text, invalid encoding, missing files, oversized inputs and hashes without exporting transcript text. Normalized hashes use Unicode NFKC followed by whitespace collapse, version 1. Identical transcript hashes do not automatically merge recordings or prove identity. Source speaker, output voice and reference identity remain unknown pending publisher evidence or controlled review. ChatterboxTTS remains protected as the proposed final family.

Results are saved under a new `data/manifests/phase2_pairs/<run-id>/` directory. `ready_for_review` means the computational audit finished; it does not verify spoken identity or grant scientific admission. The earlier cleanup was reported by the user; audio bytes have not been rehashed since that cleanup. A Python version difference from the historical P1-13 environment remains relevant for later numerical training, not a claim of this pairing audit.
